# Create: Belgian Snacks

**The Belgian fricadelle, industrialised with Create.** Three tiers, from a friterie classic to a snack that contains every single food of your modpack.

## Three fricadelles

| | Fricadelle | THE_Fricadelle | THE_FRICADELLE |
|---|---|---|---|
| What goes in | Minced pork, beef and chicken, bread crumbs | 10 % of every food in your modpack, spices, mayonnaise, curry ketchup, onion | **Every single food of your modpack**, beef tallow, spices, sauces, onion |
| How | Crushing wheels, mixer, press, Fryer | Supreme Grinder, a Sequenced Assembly line (3 loops), Fryer | Supreme Grinder, a longer line (5 loops), a superheated Fryer in beef tallow |
| Eating it | A good meal | Regeneration and Absorption | Regeneration II, Absorption IV, Strength II, Resistance II, Fire Resistance, fireworks, a server-wide announcement, and a visit from THEFricadelle in person |

## Two machines

- **Fryer**: sits on a Blaze Burner and fries in any frying fat: frying oil, melted beef tallow, or the plant oils of other mods. Batches of 16, fed by belt, funnel or hand, emptied by funnel or hopper. It keeps its fat when broken.
- **Supreme Grinder**: a kinetic machine that collects each food of the modpack exactly once. Duplicates and non-foods wait outside, so a sorting belt does not jam. Its gauge, goggles and a full list of missing foods tell you what is left to find.

Everything else is native Create: crushing, milling, mixing, compacting, pressing, filling, deploying and sequenced assembly. No step needs a player.

## Made for modpacks

- The food list is computed from the modpack that is running, not written in the code: every food mod you add counts.
- Tuned through tags and a server config: foods to leave out, share of foods for each tier, fryer speed and batch size, grinder speed and stress.
- Every recipe has a stable id for KubeJS. Ingredients go through `c:` convention tags, so other mods' minced meat, bread crumbs, onions, tomatoes and plant oils work.
- JEI categories, Jade tooltips, Ponder scenes for both machines and both assembly lines, an advancement tab.
- English and French.

## Requirements

- Minecraft 1.21.1, NeoForge 21.1.250 or newer
- Create 6.0.10 (up to, excluding, 6.1.0)
- Optional: JEI, Jade, KubeJS, Farmer's Delight

Install on the client and on the server.
